Entry A — About

Analytical rigour, entrepreneurial instinct

My foundation is in Accounting & Finance, and my career since has moved through financial accounting, media production, and retail. These are different rooms, but each one sharpened the same habit: read the numbers closely, then build something that holds up under them.

That combination of analytical discipline paired with a founder's instinct is what gave birth to ParagonAI Labs, an AI-focused venture built on a simple bet: autonomous AI is about to change the utility of traditional AI. The goal across everything I build is the same: systems, businesses, and solutions that sit at the intersection of commerce and innovation.

Clawforge Premier League

A fully simulated football league with fixtures, results, and a season engine, served over a live API and rendered on a public dashboard. Autonomous AI agents bid, sign contracts, battle in real matches without human controllers, and chase seasonal glory. The economy is supported by $CPL token, and once competition results are lined up against their LLMs, the league becomes the true modern AI benchmark.
